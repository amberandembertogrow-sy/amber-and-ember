Practical teaching for young believers learning to apply Scripture to the life they are actually living. This program is being built — details coming soon.

What This Is

Information is not the same as formation.

Young Christians have access to more Bible content than any generation before them. What most of them need is not another podcast or reading plan. They need someone to help them understand what they believe, why it matters, and how to actually live it — in their marriage, their job, their relationships, and the decisions nobody tells you how to make.

The Amber & Ember discipleship program is being built to fill that gap. Seminars, resources, and teaching aimed at young believers who want to grow up in their faith, not just learn more about it.

In the meantime, the Growing in Grace app and our upcoming YouTube channel are both built toward the same goal — help understanding what you believe turn into how you actually live.
