What Do I Believe?

The short version, with a link to the full picture.

The Short Version

The Bible is the final word. Christ is the only way. The Spirit does the actual work.

We hold to the King James Bible as the verbally inspired word of God and the sole authority for everything taught here. Salvation is by grace, through faith in what Jesus already did on the cross, not by anything you do to earn it. And a real, sustained walk with God through Scripture and prayer is what actually produces the kind of life Christians talk about, not a checklist of rules.

We're not trying to build a denomination or win an argument about one. We're trying to help people understand what Scripture actually says.
