Serve

Join the Team

A crew that shows up where the need actually is — building, cleaning, serving, and preaching Christ.

What the Team Does

Whatever the need actually is, that's the job.

This is a crew that goes where the need actually is. Construction and repair. Disaster relief. Cleaning and general upkeep on a property that's gotten away from a small congregation. Outreach to the surrounding community. One-off events a church needs extra hands for. Even teaching — seminars, when that's what's needed instead of a hammer.

That list isn't fixed. If a church, ministry, or like-minded organization has a real need and we have a way to meet it, that's the job, whatever shape it takes.

Who's On It

Young men and women, ages fifteen to twenty-five, led by a mature mentor.

The crew serves alongside a mature couple or leader who mentors the whole team the entire time they're serving — not just running the job site, but watching over how it's run. Because the crew is young and mixed, we take real care in how it's built: clear boundaries around how men and women on the team interact, and clear expectations communicated to parents before anyone under twenty-five is sent out. We'd rather move slower here than get it wrong.

How We Show Up

We're not there to fix your church. We're there to help it.

We're working on somebody else's ground, in somebody else's church or organization. We won't always agree on every point of doctrine, and that's fine — we're not there to correct anybody or take over what they're already doing. We show up to build, to give, and to preach Christ. Nothing more, nothing less.
