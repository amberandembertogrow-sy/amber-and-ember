Growing in Grace

Privacy Policy

How the Growing in Grace app handles your information, and how to delete it.

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Who we are

Growing in Grace ("the App") is a Bible study and discipleship app made by Amber and Ember ("we", "us"). This policy covers the App on Android and on the web at app.amberandember.org. It explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it.

2. What we collect

We only collect what the App needs to work for you:

  • Account details: your email address and a password, which we store only as a scrambled (hashed) value. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture link, and Google account ID from Google.
  • Profile details you choose to add: first and last name, a short bio, a location, and a favorite verse.
  • What you write in the App: prayers and prayer lists, Prayer Wall posts and updates, morning intentions, evening reflections, journal entries, notes, highlights, bookmarks, lesson answers, chats with Frank, and the studies and reading plans you ask the App to build.
  • Your activity in the App: reading history and progress, streaks, badges, and completed studies.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, your device or browser gives us a push address and keys so we can send them, along with the times you pick.
  • Reports: if you report a Prayer Wall post, we keep the report and the reason you gave.
  • Technical data: your IP address is held for up to 15 minutes to limit password reset requests. A sign-in cookie keeps you signed in. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not collect your location from your device, your contacts, or your photos.

3. What other people can see

Most of what you write is private to your account. Two features are shared on purpose:

  • The Prayer Wall is public. Anyone who opens it can see your post, its updates, and your first name. If you have not set a first name, the part of your email address before the @ is shown instead.
  • Shared prayer lists and shared prayers can be seen by the people you share them with.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Run the App and keep your content in your account
  • Send the notifications you turned on and password reset emails you ask for
  • Answer your questions to Frank and build the studies and reading plans you request
  • Review reported Prayer Wall posts and keep the community safe
  • Keep the App secure and fix problems
  • Meet legal obligations

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Frank and other AI features

Frank, the in-app study assistant, and the App's AI-built studies and reading plans run on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic. When you chat with Frank, your messages and the earlier messages in that chat are sent to Anthropic to produce a reply. If you ask Frank about your own growth, the evening reflection entries for the period you choose are sent as well. When you ask for a custom study or reading plan, the topic you enter is sent.

Anthropic processes this data to return a response to the App. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, it does not use this data to train its models. Please do not share anything with Frank you would not want processed this way.

6. Service providers

These companies handle data for us, only to run the App:

  • Render: hosts the App and its database in the United States
  • Anthropic: powers Frank and AI-built studies, as described above
  • Google: Google sign-in, if you choose it
  • Resend: delivers password reset emails
  • Your device or browser's push service (such as Google, Apple, Mozilla, or Microsoft): delivers notifications you turn on

We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

7. How long we keep it, and how to delete it

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete single items, such as notes, prayers, Frank chats, and generated studies, at any time in the App.

To delete your whole account, open More and tap Delete Account, or go to app.amberandember.org/delete-account. Deletion is permanent and takes effect right away. It removes your account and the data tied to it, including your profile, prayers and prayer lists, Prayer Wall posts, journals and reflections, notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading history, streaks, Frank chats, notification settings, and any linked Google sign-in.

If you can't sign in, email office@amberandember.org from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

Shared Bible and devotional content that is not tied to your account stays in the App. Copies in our hosting provider's backups and server logs may remain for a limited time before they are overwritten.

8. Security

The App uses encrypted connections (HTTPS), stores passwords only as hashes, and limits who can reach the database. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Our app is available to users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information without verified parental consent, we will delete it as soon as possible.

If you believe we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us at office@amberandember.org

10. Your choices

You can:

  • Edit or clear your profile details in the App
  • Turn notifications off in the App or in your device settings
  • Delete any item you created, or your whole account
  • Ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it by emailing us

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the App.
