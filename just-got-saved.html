I Just Got Saved

Something real happened. Here's what it means, and what to actually do next.

What Just Happened

You didn't just decide to be better. You were made new.

Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new. That's 2 Corinthians 5:17, and it's not a metaphor for trying harder. Something actually changed. The debt sin held over you was paid by Christ on the cross, and the Spirit of God now lives in you. You didn't clean yourself up and then get saved. You got saved, and now the cleaning up happens from the inside out.

That doesn't mean you'll feel different every day, or that the fight against sin is over. It means the outcome is settled. You're not working toward acceptance with God. You're working from it.

What To Do Now

Four things that actually matter in the first weeks.

Open your Bible

Not the whole thing at once. Start here for where to actually begin.

Talk to God

Prayer isn't a performance. Here's how to start, honestly.

Find a local church

This isn't optional in Scripture. You need people who will actually know you, not just this website.

Tell someone

Say it out loud to a person you trust. It makes it real in a way that stays private conviction never does.
