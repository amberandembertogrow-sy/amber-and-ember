Support

Church Support

Practical help for pastors and ministries doing good work with limited resources. Not active yet — this is the direction we're building toward.

The Need

A lot of pastors are doing good work with very little.

Most congregations are small. The pastor is doing the preaching, the counseling, the administration, and often the physical upkeep of the building. When something breaks or a project needs hands, there is no budget and no crew. The work slows down or stops. That is where we come in.

Amber & Ember is building toward construction crews, outreach trips, direct funding, and practical support for needs that fall outside what a congregation can cover on its own. This arm isn't live yet — we're targeting launch by the end of 2027 — but we're building it now. The goal is not to build something new — it is to get behind what God is already doing in local churches and give it room to grow.

What We're Building

How we plan to help

This isn't a fixed list — if a church or like-minded organization has a real need and we have a way to meet it, we want to hear about it, whatever shape it takes. These three are the starting point:

Construction & Repair

A team to handle physical work — building repairs, renovations, and projects that require skilled labor the church cannot afford to hire.

Outreach

A crew — ages fifteen to twenty-five, led by a mature mentor — that stands alongside a ministry for a stretch of time to help get the gospel in front of more people.

Direct Provision

A real, specific need we're in a position to meet. Support, not a substitute for what your own congregation's tithes and offerings should already cover.

Get Involved

This initiative is being built out.

If you are a pastor or organizational leader with a need, tell us now and we'll reach out as this arm comes online. If you want to be part of a future crew instead, let us know through Get Involved.
