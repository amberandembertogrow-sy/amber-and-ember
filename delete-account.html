Growing in Grace

Delete your account

Growing in Grace is a Bible study app by Amber & Ember. You can delete your account and its data at any time.

Delete it in the app

  1. Sign in to Growing in Grace on your phone or at app.amberandember.org/delete-account.
  2. In the app, open More and tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm by typing DELETE or your account email. Deletion is permanent and happens right away.

Can't sign in?

Email office@amberandember.org from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your account and profile, prayers and prayer lists, Prayer Wall posts and updates, journals and reflections, notes, highlights, bookmarks, reading history, streaks and badges, Frank chats, generated studies and reading plans, notification settings, and any linked Google sign-in.

What is kept

Shared Bible and devotional content that is not tied to your account stays in the app. Copies in our hosting provider's backups and server logs may remain for a limited time before they are overwritten. We do not keep account records in the app's database after deletion.
