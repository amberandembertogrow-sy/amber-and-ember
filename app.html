What It Does

Scripture that reads like a conversation, not a lecture

The app is built around daily engagement with Scripture, grounded in the text itself, not recycled Sunday school answers. Frank, the in-app study assistant, is live and helps you dig into a passage right where you're reading it.

A full Bible reader, with daily devotionals woven into the text where they belong
